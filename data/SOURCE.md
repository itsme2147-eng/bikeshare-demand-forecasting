# Data source

**Bike Sharing Dataset** (hourly), Capital Bikeshare system, Washington DC, 2011-2012. 17,379 hourly records, 17 columns.

- Creators: Hadi Fanaee-T and Joao Gama (LIAAD, University of Porto)
- Citation: Fanaee-T, H. & Gama, J. (2013). Event labeling combining ensemble detectors and background knowledge. Progress in Artificial Intelligence. UCI Machine Learning Repository, https://doi.org/10.24432/C5W894
- Rental data: Capital Bikeshare public system data; weather: freemeteo.com; holidays: DC government schedule
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)
- UCI page: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/275/bike+sharing+dataset
- Copies used (verified identical): https://raw.githubusercontent.com/muditp19/UCI_Bike-sharing-dataset/master/hour.csv; https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ClaudiaCartaya/Bike-sharing/master/hour.csv
- SHA-256: e03de4ee4ef4dc376ac6e04bf829673c6269e8eba5c60fa121640fa2f829504f

The raw file is kept unmodified; cleaning happens in `bikeshare.data`.
